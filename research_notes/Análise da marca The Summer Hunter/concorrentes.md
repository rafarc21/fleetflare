## 1. What niche, audience and geography does The Summer Hunter serve?

### Takeaway
The Summer Hunter (TSH) is a **Brazilian, Portuguese-language** lifestyle media brand founded in June 2014 by journalist Ricardo Moreno. Its tagline is "o lado solar da vida" (the sunny side of life). It covers authorial and slow travel (mainly Brazil), culture, wellbeing, gastronomy, reading and "solar" trends. The audience is young-adult and adult urban Brazilians (São Paulo and Rio as hubs) who are interested in lifestyle and travel. TSH is not just a newsletter. It is a multi-channel content platform and **creative studio for brands**, and the newsletter (on beehiiv, plus a Substack presence) is one piece of a "solar system" that also includes Instagram, a print magazine, podcasts, events and expeditions.

### Inferences
- The core reader need is **inspiration and curation for a slower, "sunny", experience-led lifestyle**: where to travel in Brazil, what to read, eat and attend, and how to live better. It is not deal hunting or news. [inference]
- The business is primarily **B2B branded content and a creative studio**. Audience products (newsletter, magazine, club, podcast) build reach and credibility that the studio sells to brands. Sponsor tags in issue titles (Samsung, B3, a Costa Rica destination) support this. Reader revenue is minor: magazine sales, a possible paid Substack tier and paid expeditions. [inference]
- Geography is national Brazil, Portuguese-language, with a São Paulo/Rio core. International destinations appear as sponsored or occasional content. [inference]
- The name and tagline are in English, but all content is in Portuguese. This is not the Portuguese-from-Portugal market. [inference from all sources]

## 2. Who are the main direct competitors, and who are the indirect competitors or substitutes?

### Takeaway
No single Brazilian newsletter matches TSH's exact mix of solar lifestyle, slow travel in Brazil, culture, a creative studio, print and events. The closest competitors split into three groups:
- **Brazilian lifestyle and culture newsletters and digital magazines:** Gama Revista, Tá Todo Mundo Tentando, Newsletter da Zelia, MargeM, Revista Diverso.
- **Brazilian travel newsletters and blogs:** Viaje na Viagem, MIPYMAG, Viajante, Caderno de Viagem.
- **Legacy lifestyle publishers that also sell branded content:** Trip Editora (Trip/Tpm).

Indirect substitutes include travel-deal and booking media (Melhores Destinos, Trivva), big portals and city guides (Time Out, Catraca Livre, Casa Vogue and others), travel and lifestyle Instagram/TikTok creators, and Substack's discovery feed.

### Cited Findings: direct competitors

**A. Gama Revista (Grupo Nexo), [gamarevista.uol.com.br]**
- **Offer and model:** Launched in 2020 by Grupo Nexo as a weekly online lifestyle and culture magazine. It is **not paywalled** and is funded by advertising. Sporadic print issues, events and workshops were planned and then suspended due to Covid-19. — [Acontecendo Aqui (2020)](https://acontecendoaqui.com.br/?p=102765)
- **Difference from TSH:** Gama is more journalistic and thematic (weekly "big question" editions) and is backed by a news group. TSH is more aspirational and travel-led, and is run as a creative studio. [inference]
- **Gap:** No current audience or newsletter numbers found. The 2020 data may be outdated.

**B. Tá Todo Mundo Tentando (Gaía Passarelli), Substack**
- **Offer:** A weekly "Guia Pauliceia" of São Paulo cultural tips, a Friday chronicle and a monthly themed send. It has **20k+ subscribers** according to Noize and **22k+** according to Exame. Gaía Passarelli was hired to lead Substack's partnerships in Brazil. — [NOIZE](https://www.noize.com.br/10-newsletter-musica-cultura); [Exame](https://exame.com/insight/avaliado-em-mais-de-us-1-bi-substack-tem-um-plano-para-o-brasil-e-ele-inclui-c-levels/p)
- **Difference from TSH:** A personal, author-voice culture newsletter focused on São Paulo. It overlaps with TSH's weekend and culture curation but has no travel focus and no studio. [inference]

**C. Ladrilho Hidráulico (Guilherme Werneck)**
- **Offer:** A free weekly newsletter of São Paulo cultural tips. — [NOIZE](https://www.noize.com.br/10-newsletter-musica-cultura)

**D. Newsletter da Zelia, [newsletterdazelia.com.br]**
- **Offer:** Presents itself as a reference in fashion, beauty and pop culture, and has a "lifestyle" tag. It appears in beehiiv's list of top Brazilian newsletters. — [Newsletter da Zelia, lifestyle tag](https://www.newsletterdazelia.com.br/t/lifestyle); [beehiiv blog](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/top-10-brazilian-newsletters-to-subscribe-to)
- **Difference from TSH:** Leans toward fashion and beauty rather than travel and a solar lifestyle. Both are beehiiv-native Brazilian lifestyle newsletters, so they compete for the same sponsors. [inference]
- **Gap:** Subscriber numbers not found.

**E. MargeM (Thiago Ney) and Farofafá**
- **Offer:** MargeM goes out on Fridays and mixes original content with cultural curation. Farofafá covers Brazilian culture through shows, interviews and reviews. — [ELLE Brasil](https://elle.com.br/cultura/tem-carta-para-voce-newsletters-que-valem-a-pena-assinar)

**F. Viaje na Viagem (Ricardo Freire), [viajenaviagem.com]**
- **Offer:** A Brazilian travel site founded at the end of 2004. Freire also writes a weekly Estadão column, hosts a podcast ("Sua Viagem") and has columns on BandNews FM and in Revista da Gol.
- **Audience:** About 612k Instagram followers and about 234.5k on X (snapshot date unknown). An older Projeto Draft profile, roughly seven years old, reported 1.3M unique visitors and 3.6M page views in one month. — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/viajenaviagem/); [X](https://x.com/viajenaviagem); [Projeto Draft](https://www.projetodraft.com/a-reinvencao-pessoal-de-ricardo-freire-e-a-nova-forma-de-produzir-conteudo-de-turismo-na-web/)
- **Difference from TSH:** Practical, service-oriented travel journalism ("where to stay, how to get there") with a broader, older audience. TSH is about mood, aspiration and slow travel. Viaje na Viagem is the strongest Brazilian authority in travel SEO and social. [inference]

**G. MIPYMAG, [mipymag.com.br]**
- **Offer:** A free weekly newsletter on travel and experiences, also available via Substack. It claims "mais de 12 mil assinantes". [verified-snippet, self-reported] — [MIPYMAG](https://mipymag.com.br/newsletter/)
- **Difference from TSH:** Its positioning (travel plus experiences) is the closest to TSH's travel newsletter, but its scale and brand ecosystem are smaller. [inference]

**H. Revista Diverso (Substack)**
- **Offer:** A luxury lifestyle publication with a travel section, for example on destinations that shaped Brazilian identity. It has "hundreds of subscribers". — [Revista Diverso](https://newsletter.revistadiverso.com.br/t/viagem)

**I. Viajante, Let's Fly Away, NewZ da NAZA and Caderno de Viagem**
- **Offer:** Viajante is a weekly newsletter on culture and destinations. Let's Fly Away is monthly, with one theme per edition. NewZ da NAZA covers culture, travel, gastronomy and wellbeing, which closely mirrors TSH's mix. Caderno de Viagem is a free, fortnightly, personal travel newsletter on Substack. — [Manual do Usuário newsletter directory](https://manualdousuario.net/newsletters-brasileiras/); [Caderno de Viagem](https://cadernodeviagem.substack.com/recommendations)
- **Gap:** No size data.

**J. Trip Editora (Revista Trip and Tpm), [revistatrip.uol.com.br]**
- **Offer:** Trip was founded in 1986 with surf on its first cover. It is a lifestyle and behaviour brand with a long history of custom publishing for brands: Audi Magazine, Revista Gol, Itaú Personnalité, Cidade Jardim, FAAP and Seara's "Hoje Tem Frango".
- **Commercial:** In October 2025, the influencer agency **Brunch** took over commercial management of Trip and Tpm. — [Wikipedia, Trip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trip_(magazine)); [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/aos-30-trip-alimenta-projetos-customizados-para-marcas); [Meio & Mensagem, Brunch](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/editora-trip-escolhe-brunch-como-parceira-comercial)
- **Difference from TSH:** Trip is the incumbent with the most similar DNA (sun, surf, behaviour, a premium print feel) and the same business model (branded content and custom publishing). TSH is younger, digital and newsletter-first, and cheaper and more nimble for brands. **This is TSH's most direct competitor for brand budgets.** [inference]

### Cited Findings: indirect competitors and substitutes
- **Trivva:** A hotel and resort subscription service ("turismo por assinatura") with expert-curated stays in Brazil and abroad. It claims 2,400 subscribers. It substitutes for TSH's hotel and destination curation with a transactional product. — [Trivva](https://trivva.com.br/)
- **Substack Travel discovery and international curators:** Substack's travel leaderboard and hotel curators such as Happy Hoteling compete for the same "curated stays" attention among bilingual readers. — [Substack Top Travel](https://substack.com/top/travel)
- **Samsung TV Plus "Destinos que Inspiram":** A FAST-channel curation of Brazilian travel portraits. Samsung is also a TSH newsletter sponsor, which shows brands building their own travel content. — [Samsung Newsroom BR](https://news.samsung.com/br/destinos-que-inspiram-samsung-tv-plus-apresenta-curadoria-dedicada-aos-retratos-do-brasil)
- **ELLE Brasil:** Lists culture newsletters such as Rodrigo Salem's "Desafiador do Desconhecido" (free and paid tiers), which shows the competitive set for "what to watch, read and listen to" curation. — [ELLE Brasil](https://elle.com.br/cultura/tem-carta-para-voce-newsletters-que-valem-a-pena-assinar)

### Inferences
- **Other likely indirect competitors (not researched in depth; no sources gathered):**
  - Melhores Destinos (travel deals, very large audience)
  - Catraca Livre, Time Out, Guia Folha and Veja Comer & Beber (city and leisure guides)
  - Casa Vogue, Vogue and ELLE Brasil (lifestyle and design)
  - Travel and lifestyle creators on Instagram and TikTok
  - Booking and inspiration apps (Airbnb Experiences, Google Travel)
  - AI travel planners (ChatGPT and similar)
- At about 430k Instagram followers, TSH's social reach is in the same league as Viaje na Viagem's Instagram (about 612k). It is larger than most independent Brazilian newsletters, which typically range from hundreds to about 20k subscribers.

## 3. How do competitors position themselves and monetize?

### Takeaway
The Brazilian lifestyle and travel newsletter scene is mostly **free plus advertising or sponsorships**. Paid tiers appear mainly among personal culture newsletters on Substack. The larger players (Trip, TSH, Viaje na Viagem) earn from **branded content and custom projects**, plus columns and partnerships. TSH stands out for a diversified stack: sponsored editions, a creative studio, a paid print magazine, events and expeditions, and a free club for engagement.

### Cited Findings
- **Gama Revista:** Free, ad-funded, no paywall. — [Acontecendo Aqui](https://acontecendoaqui.com.br/?p=102765)
- **Trip Editora:** Custom publishing for brands. Commercial management was outsourced to influencer agency Brunch in October 2025. — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/editora-trip-escolhe-brunch-como-parceira-comercial); [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/editora-trip-investe-em-criacao-para-marcas)
- **Desafiador do Desconhecido (Rodrigo Salem):** Paid plans plus a free plan. — [ELLE Brasil](https://elle.com.br/cultura/tem-carta-para-voce-newsletters-que-valem-a-pena-assinar)
- **MIPYMAG and Ladrilho Hidráulico:** Free weekly newsletters. — [MIPYMAG](https://mipymag.com.br/newsletter/); [NOIZE](https://www.noize.com.br/10-newsletter-musica-cultura)
- **TSH:** Sponsored issues (for example [SAMSUNG] and [B3]), brand partners (Air France, Visa, Corona, Gol), magazine sales (3,000-copy print run), expeditions, and a free benefits club with festival tickets. — sources in Section 1
- **Substack fees for Brazil:** One secondary source says Brazil-based creators with a Stripe account can enable paid subscriptions "currently" without Substack's 10% fee. [unverified secondary] — [Dinheiro da Minha Empresa](https://dinheirodaminhaempresa.com/reaproveitar/substack-brasil-newsletter-pj/)
- **Paid conversion benchmark:** A generic benchmark of about 10% of sign-ups paying is cited in Brazilian coverage. [generic, low reliability] — [Nosso Meio](https://nossomeio.com.br/newsletters-retornam-a-preferencia-do-leitor-e-ganha-modelo-de-negocio-monetizado-por-assinatura/)

### Inferences
- Tone positioning [inference]:
  - TSH: optimistic, "solar", aspirational and well-being oriented, aimed at young creative urban Brazilians.
  - Viaje na Viagem: practical, witty and expert.
  - Trip: countercultural and behavioural, premium.
  - Gama: reflective and journalistic.
  - Personal Substacks (TTMT and others): intimate, first-person.
- TSH competes for **brand budgets** (against Trip, influencer agencies and creators) more than for **reader subscriptions**.

## 4. Where are the gaps and differentiation opportunities for The Summer Hunter?

### Takeaway
TSH's strengths are a clear, ownable emotional territory ("o lado solar da vida"), an omnichannel ecosystem (print, podcast, events, expeditions) and an established branded-studio model. Its opportunities are to monetize its loyal core directly, own "slow travel in Brazil" as a searchable and service vertical, publish audited audience data, and productize experiences, ideally before Trip and Brunch, creators or AI travel tools capture those budgets.

### Cited Findings
- Expeditions are being expanded after the success of the Amazon edition. — [AdNews](https://adnews.com.br/post/the-summer-hunter-lanca-expedicao-tsh-com-imersao-na-amazonia); [Live MKT News](https://livemarketing.com.br/the-summer-hunter-expande-braco-de-experiencias-solares)
- The free benefits club has more than 1,500 members. That is small compared with about 430k Instagram followers. — [Fast Company Brasil](https://fastcompanybrasil.com/5-perguntas/5-perguntas-para-ricardo-moreno-fundador-do-the-summer-hunter/); [AdNews](https://adnews.com.br/post/the-summer-hunter-lanca-expedicao-tsh-com-imersao-na-amazonia)
- Trivva shows demand for paid, curated Brazilian hotel experiences (2,400 subscribers). — [Trivva](https://trivva.com.br/)
- Substack is investing in Brazil (partnerships lead hired, recommendation network). — [Exame](https://exame.com/insight/avaliado-em-mais-de-us-1-bi-substack-tem-um-plano-para-o-brasil-e-ele-inclui-c-levels/p)

### Inferences
- **Reader revenue gap:** Most of TSH's revenue appears to be brand-driven. A paid "Clube Solar" tier is a natural test, offering destination guides, member rates at partner hotels and early access to expeditions. Club and Instagram figures suggest a large unconverted audience. [inference]
- **Service depth:** The Wednesday "miniguias pelo Brasil" could become an evergreen, searchable guide library (SEO and GEO for AI answers) to compete with Viaje na Viagem's authority. [inference]
- **Data transparency:** Competitors and TSH alike lack public newsletter metrics. A transparent media kit (subscribers, opens, demographics) would differentiate TSH with brands, especially against Trip, whose sales now go through an agency. [inference]
- **Experiences and community:** Few newsletter competitors run physical expeditions, print and events. This makes TSH's best moat "media plus experiences", similar to international models such as Monocle and Cereal. [inference]
- **Bilingual and international:** The Print Pause Lisboa presence and the English brand name suggest potential for an English or Portuguese-from-Portugal edition aimed at foreigners interested in Brazil, an under-served space. [inference]
- **Risk:** Overlap with many free culture and São Paulo newsletters dilutes the Friday "deep dive" slot. Differentiation should stay anchored in travel and the solar lifestyle. [inference]

### Gaps
- No reader research or engagement data to confirm which TSH products resonate most.

## 5. What market trends affect this niche?

### Takeaway
Newsletters are growing and becoming more monetizable. beehiiv is scaling its ad network and paid subscriptions, Substack is actively courting Brazilian creators, and brands are shifting budgets to creators and owned content. Brazil's market is still early and experimental, and most reported figures are secondary.

### Cited Findings
- **beehiiv report:** "The State of Newsletters 2026" was published in early January 2026. — [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/the-state-of-newsletters-2026)
- **beehiiv platform figures (secondary, unverified against the report):**
  - About 28B emails sent in 2025 to 255M+ unique readers. — [blog.mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=5742); [beehiiv "we analyzed 28 billion emails"](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/we-analyzed-28-billion-emails)
  - Average open rate of 41.24% in 2025, up from 37.98% in 2024. Paid subscription revenue of $19M in 2025, up 138% from $8M. — [Voxbooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/newsletter-statistics-2026)
- **beehiiv ad network:** Pays out more than $1M a month to publishers. beehiiv planned to double its ad sales team. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/beehiiv-doubles-ad-sales-team-newsletter-monetization-heats-up); [Media Confidential](https://mediaconfidential.blogspot.com/2026/01/substack-challenger-beehiiv-expects.html?m=1)
- **Substack in Brazil:** Hired Gaía Passarelli to lead partnerships in Brazil. It does not disclose Brazilian user numbers. — [Exame](https://exame.com/insight/avaliado-em-mais-de-us-1-bi-substack-tem-um-plano-para-o-brasil-e-ele-inclui-c-levels/p)
- **Brazilian sentiment:** Commentary says Substack in Brazil is still seen more as a space for experimentation than a consolidated professional model ("virou negócio nos EUA e refúgio no Brasil"). — [Indistraível](https://indistraivel.substack.com/p/por-que-o-substack-virou-negocio); [Vanessa Mendes](https://vanessamendes2.substack.com/p/31-me-conecte-com-quem-achou-que)
- **Agencies and legacy media:** Legacy lifestyle media are outsourcing commercial operations to influencer agencies (Trip to Brunch, October 2025), a sign that budgets are converging on creators. — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/editora-trip-escolhe-brunch-como-parceira-comercial)
- **TSH strategy:** TSH is betting on print and podcasts to deepen audience connection (2025). — [Alex Ferraz](https://alexferraz.com.br/2025/03/18/the-summer-hunter-aposta-no-impresso-e-no-podcast-para-revolucionar-sua-conexao-com-o-publico/)

### Inferences
- The beehiiv ad network and Boosts could add programmatic revenue on top of TSH's direct sponsorships. Since TSH sells premium branded packages, it should weigh this against brand-safety and premium positioning. [inference]
- The rise of AI search and AI travel planners threatens commodity "where to go" content. TSH's authorial voice, print and real-world experiences are defensible against it. [inference]

### Gaps
- No Brazil-specific newsletter market size data, and no figures on beehiiv adoption in Portuguese.
- I could not open beehiiv's full 2026 report, so all its figures are second-hand.
