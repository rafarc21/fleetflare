## Who founded and runs The Summer Hunter, and what is their background?

### Takeaway
TSH was founded by Brazilian journalist **Ricardo Moreno**, who is its founder and publisher. **German Carmona** is his partner (sócio) and runs the growth and new-business side; some sources also call him a co-founder. Moreno says the brand began in June 2014. Other sources give 2013 (when the idea came) and 2015.

### Cited Findings
- Ricardo Moreno is described as "fundador e publisher do The Summer Hunter" and as a journalist. Voenews names Moreno and German Carmona as "os fundadores" when it reports that they presented a new approach to promoting Brazilian tourism at an Embratur panel. — [Voenews](https://voenews.com.br/the-summer-hunter-mostra-novo-jeito-de-comunicar-o-turismo-do-brasil/)
- Origin date: Moreno told Fast Company Brasil that the brand "surgiu quase 10 anos atrás, em junho de 2014, com o discurso de eterno verão", the idea that it is always summer somewhere in the world. He credits that angle with its growth. — [Fast Company Brasil, "5 perguntas para Ricardo Moreno"](https://fastcompanybrasil.com/5-perguntas/5-perguntas-para-ricardo-moreno-fundador-do-the-summer-hunter/) (the article dates to about 2024, since it says "almost 10 years")

## What exactly does the brand do and publish, and what is its core promise to readers?

### Takeaway
TSH calls itself "o lado solar da vida" (the sunny side of life). It is an editorial content, cultural curation and contemporary-behaviour platform focused on Brazilian travel, leisure, culture and wellbeing, with a light, positive tone. It publishes across Instagram, a website, a twice-weekly newsletter, a podcast, an annual print magazine and live events. It also runs a branded-content studio.

### Cited Findings
- Tagline and self-description: site title "The Summer Hunter, o lado solar da vida". It presents itself as an editorial content platform, cultural curation and contemporary behaviour outlet, approaching life through a "solar" lens: light, positive and uncomplicated. — [thesummerhunter.com](https://thesummerhunter.com/); [About](https://thesummerhunter.com/about/)
- "Eterno verão" (eternal summer) concept: it is always summer somewhere in the world. — [Fast Company Brasil](https://fastcompanybrasil.com/5-perguntas/5-perguntas-para-ricardo-moreno-fundador-do-the-summer-hunter/)

## What are the products and offers, and how are they priced?

### Takeaway
The audience-facing products are **free**: newsletter, website, Instagram, podcast and a free benefits club. Direct consumer revenue comes from the **print magazine (R$149 for edition #08)** and a **merch shop** (tote bags, posters, stickers, accessories). The main money appears to come from **brands**: branded content and editorial campaigns, sponsored newsletter editions, sponsored events (Casa de Verão) and experiences (Expedição TSH), plus trend reports and creative direction. Ad sales are represented by the mediatech **Play9** (2025). No paid newsletter tier was found.

### Inferences
- The monetisation model is a **brand-funded lifestyle media business**: free distribution builds a large affluent audience, which TSH sells to advertisers (beverage brands such as Corona, Aperol, Campari, Ambev and Baden Baden; travel brands such as Gol, Air France and Accor; tech such as Samsung; payments such as Visa) through content, sponsored editions, events and trips. The magazine and merch work more as brand-building with some revenue than as the core business.
- The free club and referral scheme mainly grow and capture the first-party audience (lead generation for sponsors) rather than bring in direct revenue.
- No evidence of affiliate links, a paid community or consulting retainers, beyond "cultural intelligence/trend reports" and "creative direction", which are effectively consulting-like services.

- No paid newsletter tier found on beehiiv or Substack; could not confirm by opening the subscribe/upgrade pages.
- Revenue figures are not public.
