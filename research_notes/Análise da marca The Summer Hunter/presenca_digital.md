# The Summer Hunter: digital presence, distribution channels and reputation

Research date: 2026-11-03. **Method caveat:** every direct fetch was blocked by this session's network policy (proxy 403 / DNS refusal). That covered thesummerhunter.com, thesummerhunter.substack.com, linktr.ee, podcasts.apple.com and fastcompanybrasil.com, and Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Meta Ad Library and Reddit were not reachable either. **Everything below comes from web search result snippets and search-engine summaries**, not from reading the pages in full. Numbers are marked with their source and approximate date. Where a date had to be worked out from a relative label (for example "about 7 months ago"), it is marked as an **estimate**. To check these numbers, these hosts need to be allowed in the session's network settings.

**Brand identity, for context:** The Summer Hunter (TSH) is a **Brazilian, Portuguese-language** editorial brand, tagline "o lado solar da vida" (the sunny side of life). It is not an English-language beehiiv newsletter. It was founded by the journalist **Ricardo Moreno**. Sources disagree on the founding year: June 2014 per Fast Company Brasil, 2013 per his LinkedIn, and 2015 per another profile. It describes itself as a "plataforma de conteúdo e estúdio criativo que traduz o lado solar da vida em projetos editoriais e experiências reais pra marcas", and also as a platform for culture and contemporary behaviour that offers branded editorial campaigns and trend reports. — [Search summary of Fast Company Brasil / LinkedIn / Web Summit](https://fastcompanybrasil.com/5-perguntas/5-perguntas-para-ricardo-moreno-fundador-do-the-summer-hunter/); [thesummerhunter.com/about](https://thesummerhunter.com/about/); [LinkedIn – Ricardo Moreno](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rimoreno/)

## Which channels does the brand use, with what follower counts and engagement levels?

### Takeaway
TSH is an Instagram-first brand: about 430–440K followers, the largest channel by far. Around it sits an ecosystem of website, weekly newsletter (on a newsletter.* subdomain, plus a small Substack), podcasts (Desenrola / TSH Vozes / Drops Solares), YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, a WhatsApp club, a yearly print magazine, an online shop and in-person events. The company claims a combined reach of more than 5 million people a month. I found no reliable follower counts for TikTok, YouTube or Facebook.

## What growth/acquisition tactics are visible (referral rewards, recommendations, giveaways, collabs, ads)?

### Takeaway
Visible growth comes mainly from **brand-funded editorial campaigns and collabs**, **events and IRL experiences**, **creator expeditions**, **cross-promotion between owned channels** (podcast → magazine pre-sale, newsletter → events) and a **paid membership/benefits club**. I found no evidence of a beehiiv referral program, Boosts or recommendations, paid social ads, or giveaways, because those sources could not be accessed.

### Cited Findings
- **Creator/influencer expedition:** "Expedição TSH" took **20 creators** to the Amazon with the responsible-luxury tourism operator **Katerre** (about 2026, estimated). This is a creator-collab tactic that multiplies reach through the guests' own audiences. — [AdNews](https://adnews.com.br/post/the-summer-hunter-lanca-expedicao-tsh-com-imersao-na-amazonia); [Aurora Cultural](https://auroracultural.com/noticias/turismo-lazer/the-summer-hunter-leva-20-criadores-para-a-amazonia/)

Newsletters promote events (Casa TSH SP). — [Spotify for Creators](https://creators.spotify.com/pod/profile/desenrola-tsh/episodes/83---T-mais-difcil-conhecer-gente-nova-e33va46); [Newsletter](https://newsletter.thesummerhunter.com/p/newsletter-casa-the-summer-hunter-sp)

## Which brands sponsor or partner with it? What does that say about its audience and positioning?

### Takeaway
TSH's partners are premium lifestyle, travel, beverage and financial brands: **Baden Baden (via Ogilvy), Jeep (via Fbiz), Mastercard, Stanley, Katerre, Air France, Visa, Corona, Gol**. This positions TSH as an aspirational, urban (SP/Rio), affluent young-adult lifestyle, travel and culture outlet. Its pitch to brands is "(un)branded content": integrations that look editorial, not ads.

### Cited Findings
- **Baden Baden + Ogilvy:** TSH became **"The Winter Hunter" for 48 hours (July 3–5)**, with winter content on tourism, gastronomy, behaviour and experiences, tied to the launch of Baden Baden Coffee (beer). It also debuted TSH's first travel newsletter. *Year: a search summary said 2026; the Propmark article date was not verified.* — [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/anunciantes/baden-baden-transforma-the-summer-hunter-em-the-winter-hunter-para-celebrar-o-inverno/); [Grandes Nomes da Propaganda](https://grandesnomesdapropaganda.com.br/mercado-digital/the-winter-hunter-e-nova-acao-da-baden-baden/)
- **Mastercard and Stanley:** sponsored **Desenrola season 3** (March 2025). Mastercard had two spots; Stanley had a themed episode. — [Alex Ferraz](https://alexferraz.com.br/2025/03/18/the-summer-hunter-aposta-no-impresso-e-no-podcast-para-revolucionar-sua-conexao-com-o-publico/)
- **Jeep (agency Fbiz):** Children's Day project "Aventurinhas Sem Filtro" (day-trip itineraries), described as the start of an ongoing partnership through the summer (**older campaign, date not confirmed**). — [Marcas pelo Mundo](https://marcaspelomundo.com.br/anunciantes/jeep-e-the-summer-hunter-criam-projeto-especial-para-o-dia-das-criancas/)
- **Katerre:** partner for Expedição TSH (Amazon, about 2026). — [AdNews](https://adnews.com.br/post/the-summer-hunter-lanca-expedicao-tsh-com-imersao-na-amazonia)
- **Air France, Visa, Corona, Gol:** cited as brand partners in the Fast Company Brasil interview (about 2024). — [Fast Company Brasil](https://fastcompanybrasil.com/5-perguntas/5-perguntas-para-ricardo-moreno-fundador-do-the-summer-hunter/)
- Ambev, AccorHotels and Aperol were mentioned by one search summary as partners "in other sources", but **no specific source URL was returned**. Treat as unverified.
- **Positioning quotes (Moreno):** "o que fazemos não é branded content, mas (un)branded content" (what we do is not branded content but (un)branded content), and "quando você força a barra e tenta objetivamente vender um produto X ou Y, a audiência te abandona" (when you push too hard to sell product X or Y, the audience abandons you). — [Fast Company Brasil (via search summary)](https://fastcompanybrasil.com/5-perguntas/5-perguntas-para-ricardo-moreno-fundador-do-the-summer-hunter/)

### Inferences
- Airlines (Air France, Gol), premium payments (Visa, Mastercard), beer and premium drinks (Corona, Baden Baden), a premium SUV (Jeep), lifestyle hardware (Stanley) and luxury eco-tourism (Katerre) point to an audience of affluent, urban, 25–40-year-old Brazilians with travel and leisure spend.
- Partners often come through big agencies (Ogilvy, Fbiz). TSH is bought as a creative studio and vehicle within agency plans, not just as ad inventory.
- Revenue appears diversified: branded projects, podcast sponsorship, magazine and shop sales, events, club memberships and expeditions.

## How does SEO/search visibility look (does it rank for its niche terms, what keywords)?

### Takeaway
Brand-name search visibility is strong: a query for the domain returns the site, Substack, Instagram, shop and section pages. In US-based English search the generic phrase "The Summer Hunter newsletter" did **not** surface TSH, being crowded out by hunting newsletters. Niche keyword rankings could not be measured with the available tools.

### Cited Findings
- A search for "thesummerhunter.com" returns the homepage ("The Summer Hunter, o lado solar da vida"), /newsletter, /about, /miniguia, /vibes, /radio-veneno, the shop, the Substack and Instagram. — [Home](https://thesummerhunter.com/); [Newsletter](https://thesummerhunter.com/newsletter/); [Miniguias](https://thesummerhunter.com/miniguia/)
- An English-language search for "The Summer Hunter" newsletter (US search engine) returned **only unrelated hunting and Substack newsletters**. — [search, 2026-11-03]
- Content types likely to target search: **Miniguias** (destination mini-guides, for example travel and roteiro keywords), long-form **Vibes** features (culture and music, for example "Veneno, a rádio que nasceu pra unir todas as cenas"). — [Miniguias](https://thesummerhunter.com/miniguia/); [Radio Veneno](https://thesummerhunter.com/radio-veneno/)

### Inferences
- SEO is likely a minor acquisition channel compared with Instagram. The miniguias give the best organic potential (Portuguese long-tail travel queries such as "o que fazer em…", "roteiro…").
- The English-language brand name collides with US hunting content, which hurts discoverability outside Brazil.

### Gaps
- No keyword ranking, domain authority or traffic data. SEO tools and Google.com.br SERPs were not accessible, and the search tool is US-only.
- Whether TSH ranks for Portuguese niche terms ("newsletter de viagem", "lifestyle verão", "roteiros solares") is unverified.
